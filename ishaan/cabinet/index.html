So the cabinet DID open, at the very last second, Before I went in, I shoved the diary back in the drawer and spun the lock, my hands were shaking like crazy.

Bhaiya came in. He didn’t turn the light on. He just stood there for a while…like he was listening, STARING AT THE CABINET. I swear I stopped breathing.

Then he went to the drawer, opened it normal, took the diary out and walked to the shelf. He slid it into that empty spot

I swear I heard it click, like a latch and the whole fucking shelf swung out. Right over those scuff marks. Behind it there were stairs, going DOWN. That’s the thudding I keep hearing every evening, its him going up and down those stairs. How long has that been there?? Did Maa know??

He went down and pulled the shelf back behind him. Of the little I could hear, it sounded similar to that day’s recording…its so weird.

I was scared shitless, I ran back to my room. I took one picture through the gap before I left, I dont even know if it came out.

I’m going back. Tomorrow, when he’s out. I know its stupid but I need to know whats down there
